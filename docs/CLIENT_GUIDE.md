# VYRO — CLIENT & STORE MANAGER'S GUIDE
**Audience:** VYRO Brand Managers, Merchandisers, and Content Editors  
**Skill Level:** No coding or technical knowledge required

This guide explains how to manage your products, adjust pricing, update announcements, and edit homepage content using the standard Shopify Admin interface.

---

## 1. Managing Products & Inventory

### How to Add or Edit Products
1. Log into your Shopify Admin (`admin.shopify.com`).
2. Navigate to **Products**.
3. To edit **FLEXFIT 01**, click on the product title.
4. **Title & Description:** Keep the headline concise. Highlight key features: athletic cut, four-way stretch, reinforced seams, and pocket placement.
5. **Pricing:** Update the **Price** (e.g. ₹2,299) and **Compare-at price** (e.g. ₹2,799 to display a strikethrough discount).
6. **Images:** Upload at least 7 photos in this recommended sequence:
   - Image 1: On-model front view (used as the primary product card image)
   - Image 2: On-model back view (used as the hover image on collection pages)
   - Image 3: On-model side profile view
   - Image 4: Lifestyle wear shot in clinic or modern environment
   - Image 5: 1:1 close-up of fabric weave texture
   - Image 6: 1:1 close-up of deep hip/zippered pockets
   - Image 7: 1:1 close-up of reinforced seam or neckline construction
7. **Variants (Sizes & Colours):**
   - Ensure Option 1 is **Size** (S, M, L, XL, XXL).
   - Ensure Option 2 is **Colour** (Midnight Navy).
   - Enter your inventory quantities for each size.
   - When a size variant drops to `0`, the theme automatically marks the size chip as unavailable (struck through) and prevents customers from adding it to their bag.

## 2. Editing Homepage Content (Theme Customizer)

To edit the homepage visually:
1. Go to **Online Store > Themes**.
2. Click the green **Customize** button on the active VYRO theme.

### Announcement Bar
- Click on **Announcement Bar** in the sidebar.
- You can customize:
  - **Message:** (e.g. "Free Shipping Across India | Easy Returns")
  - **Track Order URL:** Link to your Shiprocket or tracking page.
  - **Support URL:** Link to your contact page.

### Hero Slideshow
- Click on **Hero** in the sidebar.
- You can add up to 5 slides.
- For each slide, upload an image formatted to **16:7 aspect ratio** (e.g., 1920 × 840 px).
- Update the **Headline**, **Subline**, and **CTA Button label**.

### Featured Product (FLEXFIT 01 Split Layout)
- Click on **Featured Product**.
- Select the product from your catalog (it will automatically link prices and variants).
- Upload the front, back, and pocket detail images.
- Set the **Tagline** (default: "Move. Perform. Excel.").

### The Anatomy of FLEXFIT
- Click on **FLEXFIT Anatomy**.
- Upload a clean front-facing shot of the scrub top.
- Click on any **Hotspot** block to drag or adjust its horizontal (`position_x`) and vertical (`position_y`) percentage on the garment.
- Update the feature label and explanation once lab test specifications are confirmed.

### ScentShift Technology Section
- **Important Note:** This section is an R&D brand positioning statement. Keep the status badge set to *"CURRENTLY IN DEVELOPMENT."* Do not claim availability or commercial guarantees.

### Customer Reviews
- The theme ships with an empty state placeholder.
- As genuine verified reviews come in from doctors and clinicians post-launch, click **Add Review** inside the Reviews section to add their star rating, quote, and professional title (e.g. "Dr. A. Verma, Surgical Resident").

## 3. Managing Policies & Legal Pages

1. Navigate to **Settings > Policies** in Shopify Admin.
2. Edit your legal agreements:
   - **Refund Policy:** Specify your return and exchange window and sanitary return requirements.
   - **Privacy Policy:** Required for Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, and payment processing.
   - **Terms of Service:** Standard commercial terms for India.
   - **Shipping Policy:** Detail delivery timeframes (5–8 days across major metros).
3. The VYRO theme footer links directly to these policies automatically.
